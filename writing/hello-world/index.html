Hello, world

5 November 2026 · George Dyke

Every writing section has to start somewhere, and convention says it starts here.

This is a place for occasional writing on Earth observation, space, technology, and the systems and policies that shape them. Some pieces will explore ideas in more depth; others will accompany talks and presentations, written so the argument survives without the slides.

There is no schedule. If you would like to know when something new appears, there is an RSS feed, and I’ll share pieces on LinkedIn as they go up.
