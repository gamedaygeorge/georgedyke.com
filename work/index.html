Work & publications

Systems George has built and operated, and a curated selection of reports, papers, and handbook editions. More entries are being added as the archive is assembled.

Platforms & systems

  • CEOS Database The database of Earth observation satellites, instruments, and measurements behind the CEOS EO Handbook and CEOS reporting, used across the international EO community. George is the architect and main developer of the system in its current incarnation, working with Symbios in support of ESA and CEOS.
  • EO data explorer & API In development — a new online explorer and API for Earth observation capabilities and data, entering a private pilot in late 2026.
  • eoPortal Content management for ESA’s eoPortal satellite missions catalogue, with Symbios.

Earlier & other work

  • Canadarm2 1999–2004 · MD Robotics George’s first job in the space sector: operations analyst on the Canadarm2 programme with MD Robotics, based at the Canadian Space Agency during the arm’s deployment flights and the early construction of the International Space Station, and during the joint Canadian–US–Japanese design and simulation of the Japanese HTV free-flyer capture maneuver, procedures, crew trials, and training.
  • GameDayRitual 2004–2018 Co-founder and co-developer of GameDayRitual — an innovative (and fun!) next generation fantasy baseball and football strategy game — increasingly part time over the years, until it was wound down in 2018.
